## ☁️ Cloud Storage (Privacy-First + Huge Free Tiers)

| Service | Free Tier | Why It's Arbitrage |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **MEGA** | **20 GB** (up to 35 GB via "achievements") | Zero-knowledge encryption by default, CLI (`megacmd`), browser-based, EU-hosted |
| **Blomp** | **200 GB** | No file size limits, WebDAV support, multi-cloud backup |
| **DeGoo** | **100 GB** | Auto photo backup, but ad-supported & slower |
| **pCloud** | 10 GB (→ 10 GB more via referrals) | Lifetime plans exist, media streaming, Crypto (paid) for E2EE |
| **Proton Drive** | 5 GB (but **Proton Unlimited = 500 GB + VPN + Mail + Pass + Calendar** for $9.99/mo) | Swiss jurisdiction, full E2EE ecosystem — best value if you want the *suite* |
| **Sync.com** | 5 GB (+1 GB/referral) | Zero-knowledge, Canadian, great sharing controls |

> **Pro tip:** Stack MEGA (20 GB encrypted) + pCloud (10 GB media) + Proton (5 GB docs) = **35 GB premium encrypted storage free**.
